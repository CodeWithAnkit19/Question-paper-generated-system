About university

About Galgotias University

Galgotias University is located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university provides education across multiple disciplines and promotes teaching, research, innovation and practical learning.

The university began its academic operations during the 2011–2012 academic session and admitted its first batch in July 2011.

Its academic environment combines classroom education, practical facilities, research activities, industry interaction and student development.

🌟 Academic Environment

The university focuses on value-based education, research, innovation, outreach and sustainable practices.


Students get opportunities to participate in academic, technical, cultural, sports and innovation activities.
